# 06 - Analytics, Interactivity & Final Publishing Principles

## 1. Initial Analytics Metrics
- **Total Views**: Count of total visitor sessions.
- **Unique Visitors**: Distinct devices accessing the microsite.
- **Views Over Time**: Visual activity timeline leading up to the celebration.
- **RSVP Submissions**: Real-time count of confirmed vs. declined guests.
- **Anti-Tracking Guarantee**: Zero visitor GPS tracking, biometric logging, or third-party advertising cookies.

---

## 2. Interactive Components Runtime
- **RSVP Form**: Real-time submission handling with immediate creator dashboard updates.
- **Google Maps Embed**: Live vector maps with direct routing to native mapping apps.
- **Spotify Music Player**: Embedded audio playback directly within the public microsite.
- **UPI QR / Shagun**: Generates standard UPI payment intent routing 100% of cash gifts directly to host accounts.

---

## 3. Master Summary of Publishing Rules
1. **Verified Availability**: An Invite is published only after programmatic verification succeeds.
2. **Zero Credit Loss on Failure**: Payment or system failure preserves user drafts and refunds/protects credits.
3. **URL Permanence**: Canonical URLs remain permanent through updates, republishing, and hosting extensions.
4. **Independent Snapshots**: Published Invites are decoupled from template and element library changes.
5. **Text-Only Free Edits**: Design changes after publication require a new Invite duplicate.
6. **30-Day Recovery**: Explicitly deleted Invites remain recoverable for 30 days.
7. **No Auto-Deletion on Expiry**: Expired Invites remain in creator dashboard ready for extension.
8. **Download Gating**: High-res Image & Multi-page PDF downloads require Premium entitlement.
