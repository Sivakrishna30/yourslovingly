# 04 - Post-Publication Editing, Actions & Downloads

## 1. Post-Publication Editing Policy
To maintain stability for public visitors and physical print distribution:

### Permitted Free Text Corrections
- Event date, time, and schedule updates
- Host, bride, groom, and celebrant name corrections
- Venue names, addresses, and directions
- Minor typo and grammar fixes

### Prohibited Post-Publication Design Modifications
- Moving, resizing, rotating, or deleting canvas elements
- Changing typography fonts, weights, colors, or sizes
- Altering background artwork, theme tokens, or SVG fills
- Adding, deleting, or re-sequencing canvas pages

---

## 2. Safe Republishing Architecture
When an authorized text update is published:
- The system regenerates the static snapshot in the background.
- If republishing succeeds $\rightarrow$ New snapshot replaces the old snapshot instantly at the **same URL**.
- If republishing fails $\rightarrow$ **The previous valid published version remains live and visible.** Visitors are never exposed to broken or incomplete states.

---

## 3. Published Actions Suite
- **Copy URL**: One-touch clipboard copy.
- **Share URL**: OS-native share sheet for messaging apps and social platforms.
- **QR Code**: Instant generation of vector/raster QR codes matching the public URL.
