# 05 - Lifecycle, Hosting & Monetization Economics

## 1. Invite Lifecycle States
User-facing invite lifecycle consists of 4 clear states:
1. **DRAFT**: Editable workspace, unpublished.
2. **PUBLISHED**: Live, publicly accessible microsite.
3. **EXPIRED**: Hosting period elapsed; public visitors see "Invite Expired", but creator can extend/restore anytime.
4. **DELETED**: Moved to trash with a **30-day recovery window**. Visitors see "Invite Temporarily Unavailable". After 30 days, permanently purged.

---

## 2. Hosting Durations & Economics
- **Initial Hosting Period**: Every newly published invite begins with **15 days of live hosting** (applies across Free Basic, ₹49 Basic, ₹99 Premium, and Pack invites).
- **Hosting Extensions (Purchased as needed)**:
  - *Basic Invite Extension*: **₹49** for +30 days.
  - *Premium Invite Extension*: **₹99** for +30 days.
- **Lifetime Hosting**: **₹999** one-time purchase per invite for permanent hosting with no expiry.

---

## 3. Account Model & Draft Limits
- **No-Account Creation**: Users can choose templates, edit, and preview without logging in. Unauthenticated drafts persist in local browser storage.
- **Mandatory Sign-In for Publishing**: Requires Google Sign-In at publication time; the local draft automatically transfers into their account.
- **Draft Limit**: Free accounts can store up to **3 active drafts**. Draft limits are separate from publishing credits.

## 5. Purchase Policies & Strategic Boundaries
- **Transactional Philosophy**: "Pay only when you need something." No recurring monthly or annual subscriptions.
- **Credit Expiry**: Purchased invite credits never expire.
- **Finality**: Purchases are one-time transactions with no product-level downgrade or refund workflows.
- **Out of Scope**: Yours Lovingly is not an enterprise CMS, a social network, an unrestricted Canva clone, or a complex CRM. It is a razor-focused design-to-publishing microsite platform.
