# 01 - Product Overview & Target Audience

## 1. Product Overview
**Yours Lovingly** is a design-to-publishing platform that allows non-technical users to create beautiful digital invites and publish them as live, shareable microsites.

The primary use case is **digital event invitations**. The product also supports business and professional use cases such as:
- Digital business cards
- Business flyers
- Pamphlets
- Portfolios
- Promotional pages
- Small-business digital pages

### The Core Journey
```text
Choose Invite Type
        ↓
Choose Template
        ↓
Enter Content
        ↓
Customize Design
        ↓
Preview
        ↓
Publish
        ↓
Live Microsite
        ↓
Share / QR / Download / Interact
```

> **Core Identity**: Yours Lovingly is primarily a publishing and microsite platform, not a general-purpose graphic design application.

---

## 2. Product Vision & Core Principle
Yours Lovingly allows an ordinary person with no technical or design expertise to create a professional-looking digital invite without needing:
- Web-development or HTML/CSS knowledge
- Graphic-design expertise
- A separate hosting provider or individual domains
- Separate QR-generation, Google Maps, RSVP, or analytics software

> **Core Principle**: *Design once. Publish digitally. Share everywhere. Extend when needed. Print when needed.*

---

## 3. Problem Statement
Creating a temporary microsite for an event currently requires finding a developer, waiting for design, purchasing hosting, setting up domains, configuring maps, and maintaining the site.

Yours Lovingly simplifies this into:
$$\text{Template} + \text{Content} + \text{Customization} = \text{Published Microsite}$$

---

## 4. Target Audiences

### Primary Audience (Celebrations & Events)
People creating digital event invitations: Couples, Families, Individuals, Event organizers.
- **Event Categories**: Wedding, Engagement, Birthday, Anniversary, Housewarming, Baby Shower, Naming Ceremony, Reception, Party, and Custom Event.

### Secondary Audience (Local Businesses & Creators)
- **Local Businesses / Shops / Gyms / Boutiques**: Business cards, flyers, pamphlets, promotions, announcements, and product pages.
- **Professionals / Creators / Freelancers / Photographers**: Portfolios, personal profiles, and professional identity pages.

---

## 5. Core Value Proposition & Differentiation
- **Value Proposition**: Choose a professionally designed template, enter information, customize freely, and publish a beautiful live microsite without hosting friction.
- **Differentiation from Canva**:
  - *Canva*: Focuses on design creation for export.
  - *Yours Lovingly*: Focuses on the complete journey from design $\rightarrow$ live microsite hosting $\rightarrow$ sharing $\rightarrow$ interactive features (RSVP, Maps, Music, UPI) $\rightarrow$ simple analytics.
