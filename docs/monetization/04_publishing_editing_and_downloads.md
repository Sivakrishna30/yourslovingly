# 04 - Publishing Entitlements, Post-Publication Editing & Downloads

## 1. The Publishing Entitlement Transaction
- **Monetization Milestone**: Publishing is the primary paid event transitioning a private draft to a live microsite.
- **Verification Rule**: Payment/credit is consumed **only after public availability is verified**.
- **Failure Safe**: If publishing fails or is aborted, zero credits/payments are consumed, and the draft remains intact.

---

## 2. Post-Publication Editing Boundaries
To maintain design integrity and hosting consistency, successfully published Invites cannot undergo free visual redesigns.

### Free Text-Only Corrections Allowed
Users can update text content without repurchasing:
- Names, titles, quotes
- Dates, times, event schedules
- Venue addresses, directions text
- Typo and spelling corrections

### Prohibited Post-Publication Modifications
Users cannot freely change:
- Element coordinates, dimensions, or canvas layout
- Font families, weights, styling, or colors
- Background patterns, SVG fills, or theme palettes
- Adding, deleting, or reordering pages
- Adding new interactive components

---

## 3. Download Entitlement Matrix
- **Basic (₹49)**: No download functionality. UI displays *"Upgrade to Premium to download"*.
- **Premium (₹99)**: Full download functionality included:
  - *Single-page Invites*: High-res Image (PNG) + Vector PDF.
  - *Multi-page Invites*: Multi-page Vector PDF document.

---

## 4. Published Invite Capacity & Absence of Slot Arithmetic
- **No Complex Slot Systems**: Users are never exposed to confusing "page slot math" (e.g. 3 slots, consumed slots, replacement slots).
- **Atomic Invite-Level Transaction**: Each published Invite is purchased individually.
  - *Basic Invite Publishing*: **₹49** per published Invite (15 days live hosting).
  - *Premium Invite Publishing*: **₹99** per published Invite (15 days live hosting).
  - *Additional Basic Invites*: Purchased individually for ₹49 each.
  - *Additional Premium Invites*: Purchased individually for ₹99 each.
- **Strictly No Bundles**: There are NO Creator Packs, bulk bundles, or discounted multi-Invite packages (e.g. no ₹499 for 7, no ₹999 for 15). Every Invite is provisioned cleanly on its own transaction.
