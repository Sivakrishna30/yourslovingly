# 03 - Pricing Tiers, Upgrades & Template Monetization

## 1. Basic Invite Pricing
- **Price**: **₹49** per published Invite (purchased individually; no bundles or Creator Packs).
- **Inclusions**:
  - **15 Days of live hosting** and view validity from publication timestamp
  - Full access to standard predesigned template library
  - All standard design elements (Text, Image, SVG, Shapes, Lines, Frames, Tables, Charts)
  - Text details, Spotify music player, and photo gallery (up to 10 photos)
  - Platform watermark (*"Crafted with Yours Lovingly"*)
  - Live public microsite hosting & permanent canonical URL
  - Free & unlimited post-publication text-only corrections
  - Free and unrestricted Draft mode
- **Exclusions**: Downloadable PDF/Image output is not included.

---

## 2. Premium Invite Pricing
- **Price**: **₹99** per published Invite (purchased individually; no bundles or Creator Packs).
- **Inclusions**:
  - All Basic inclusions
  - **15 Days of live hosting** and view validity from publication timestamp
  - **Watermark-Free Experience** (platform watermark completely removed)
  - Full access to Premium template library
  - Interactive Google Maps embed + directions
  - Auto-generated Location QR Code
  - Interactive RSVP form with attendance counting & real-time guest responses
  - UPI QR / cash gift module (direct UPI payment intent routing)
  - Detailed visitor analytics (views, unique visitors, response timeline)
  - Downloadable invite assets:
    - *Single-page Invites*: High-res PNG Image + Vector PDF
    - *Multi-page Invites*: Multi-page Vector PDF document
- **Post-Publication Editing Boundary**: Premium status **does not grant unrestricted design alterations** after publication; post-publication changes remain strictly limited to text values.

---

## 3. Basic $\rightarrow$ Premium Upgrade Flow
- **Upgrade Cost**: **₹49 additional** (₹49 original + ₹49 upgrade = ₹98 ~ ₹99 total equivalent).
- **Scope**: Applied directly to the existing published Invite without requiring re-creation or URL invalidation.
- **Immediate Effect**: Removes watermark immediately and unlocks all Premium interactive capabilities (Google Maps, RSVP, UPI QR, Analytics) and Image/PDF downloads.
- **Validity Impact**: Retains existing validity duration (does not add days by itself; extension can be purchased separately).

---

## 4. Hosting Validity & Extension Pricing
- **Initial Validity**: 15 days from publication timestamp.
- **Basic 30-Day Extension**: **₹49** (+30 days added to active expiry, or reactivates expired Invite for 30 days).
- **Premium 30-Day Extension**: **₹99** (+30 days added to active expiry, or reactivates expired Invite for 30 days).
- **Lifetime Validity**: **₹999** per Invite (permanent hosting, never expires).
- **No Bundles**: Extensions and lifetime validity apply to each specific Invite individually.

---

## 5. Template Monetization & Discovery
- **Template Tiers**: Filterable via `All`, `Basic`, and `Premium`.
- **Open Preview**: Premium templates can be fully customized and previewed in draft mode for free.
- **Publish Gate**: Using a Premium template requires a Premium entitlement (₹99) upon publication.
- **Entitlement Attachment**: Premium entitlement attaches to the specific Invite artifact, not permanently to the user's account.

---

## 6. Design Element Paywall Status
- **Current Product State**: All individual decorative and structural elements (SVG artwork, ornaments, frames, shapes, fonts) are universally accessible without individual micro-transactions.
- **Deferred Feature**: Gated individual premium elements are deferred to Phase 2.
