# 05 - Drafts, Expiry, Extensions & Refund Rules

## 1. Separation of Drafts and Published Invites
- Publishing an Invite does not deduct or restrict the user's ability to create new Drafts.
- Drafts remain exploratory canvases; published Invites represent active public artifacts.

---

## 2. Expired Invites & Validity Extensions
- **Initial Hosting Period**: Every newly published Invite begins with **15 days of live hosting** and public view validity.
- **Expired State**: Occurs when the 15-day hosting duration (or purchased extension) elapses.
- **Rules of Expiry**:
  - Expiry does **not** delete the Invite.
  - Expiry does **not** convert the Invite back into a Draft.
  - Expiry does **not** alter element configurations or historical snapshots.
  - The public URL displays a polite *"Invite Expired"* screen, but the slug and data remain intact.
- **Hosting Extensions**:
  - *Basic 30-Day Extension*: **₹49**
  - *Premium 30-Day Extension*: **₹99**
  - *Lifetime Validity*: **₹999** per Invite (permanent hosting, never expires)
- **Extension Stacking & Reactivation**:
  - *Active Invites*: +30 days extends from the current expiration timestamp.
  - *Expired Invites*: +30 days reactivates the Invite for 30 days starting immediately from purchase time.
  - *Lifetime*: Sets expiration to permanent/infinite.
  - Purchasing an extension extends the live hosting period only and does not grant post-publication layout redesign capabilities.
- **Redesigning an Expired Invite**:
  - Use **"Copy to New"** $\rightarrow$ Creates a fresh Draft Invite with a new Invite ID.
  - The original expired Invite remains safely archived in the user's dashboard.

---

## 3. Strict Refund Policy & Consumption Finality
- **Consumption Milestone**: Once an Invite is successfully published, verified, and accessible, the publishing entitlement is permanently consumed.
- **Non-Refundable Scenarios**:
  - Deleting the Invite after publishing.
  - Letting the Invite expire.
  - Short live duration / early event conclusion.
  - User deciding to publish a completely different design instead.
- **Refund Exceptions**: Restricted solely to technical billing failures or duplicate payment gateway charges.

---

## 4. Monetization Boundary Protections
Yours Lovingly explicitly prohibits micro-monetization friction:
- ❌ No paywalls on resizing, moving, or aligning elements.
- ❌ No micro-charges for standard decorative SVG assets.
- ❌ No recurring monthly subscriptions forced on seasonal/one-time users.
- ❌ No bundle/pack math or page slot calculations.
- ✅ Value is captured strictly at **Publishing**, **Premium Interactivity**, **Downloads**, and **Hosting Extensions**.
